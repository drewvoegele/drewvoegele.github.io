Where it started

A card for grandma's remote

My grandma never learned a computer, and she hated asking for help. When the satellite box arrived, she started missing Wheel of Fortune, her one reward at the end of the day. So I made her a card that fit over the remote and walked her from any state to her show. She never had to ask again.

That card wasn't my career. It was my muse. The same few ideas have run through every job since.

Leadership

How I lead

Curiosity

Care

Coaching
